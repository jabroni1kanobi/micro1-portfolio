ad a really cool idea tony, I haven't fleshed it out yet but I figured I'd send you just some stuff I'm thinking about, and I think a lot of this could be relevant for the longevity of ANY plat fighter, but also specifically multiversus. The idea is a battle royale mode for multiversus, solos and duos (duos would be the most fun imo) The closest thing to date that has come to this sort of game mode is Smash run for 3ds, and every single person I've talked to over the years absolutely loved that game mode and was sad it never made a return in any form in smash or elsewhere. My idea would be this, you're tossed into a gigantic massive stage, think metroid, or castlevania levels of different hiding places, ares to explore, upgrades to find etc. You que in with your team mate, and can pick perks that are either already in the game (if you want it that way) or a set of perks that are completely unique to this battle royale mode. Similar to other battle royales, you will be able to find upgrades or additional perks from either just discovery, or doing things like Fighting actual Player versus environment challenges throughout the game mode. A cool idea for this would be as follows: You and your team mate are trying to go to kill the joker boss for the reward. The joker boss appears randomly throughout the map (along with other bosses in different areas) everyone is alerted of this boss and it has a timer counting down, the bosses always appear in an area of the map that branches off into a real stage (picture the adventure mode legend of zelda sections in melee where you have to find the triforce, but randomly you are forced into a real battlefield and have to fight link) Once the timer ticks down and the boss fight is about to start, the zone is closed off to other players. You are now locked into fighting this boss with whoever is in there and whoever survives reaps the rewards.
Maybe the boss is so difficult you're even incentivized to team up with enemies for the time being. Ideas of potential rewards? maybe mid boss fight a robin npc fights along side you, if your team survives the battle and are the only ones remaining, you now have this robin npc on your team throughout the royale until his health is depleted.
There will also be chests that spawn and work exactly the same way as the bosses, everyone is alerted to the spawn location, once the timer hits 0, everyone that is in the spawn location (always a real stage layout type of area) is then locked in and forced to fight on a real stage for the chest. I think it would be even cooler if the rewards weren't so massive that every player was gunning for it, save those rewards for the boss fights. limit chest rewards to 2 players, now mid royale you are locked into an actual 1v1 plat form fighting fight against another team, or another player (1v1 in 1s, 2v2 in teams royale) Other mechanics: Once dead in the battle royale, you aren't out just yet, you now are once again teleported to a real stage layout (maybe some heavenly looking stage or demonic to deal with being on the brink of death) And you now have to playout a real game once again, against another team or player who has also already died once in the royale. If you survive you are placed back in the battle without any of the perks or items you had gathered on your first life. As time goes on similar to other battle royales, there would be a cirlce closing in, forcing players to branch into the more confined areas of the map, it could be more chaotic where the final battle could be a mix of teams on one big map, but in my opinion I think it would be cooler to do it this way: think dark and darker, there are only so many exits available to make it out of the map. Theres enough for 10 teams of 2, if you didn't make it out you unfortunately place 10th or less. The final 10 teams are then put into a bracket style face off where they play real matches against a team, just like multiversus would normally play out. Each time a team wins, they are brought into a waiting arena where they are now able to select one new perk for their victory while they wait for the opposing team victor to also finish their match and pick their perk.
Once both teams have selected their victory perks, they are then put into another real 2v2 against the other victors. This repeats until there is only 2 teams left, all perks items remaining on both teams, and the final royale takes place. Any players that have died are able to remain in the battle as a spectator to see how things playout if they choose (picture fall guys) I think this idea in a game like multiversus if done well, could single handedly keep the game alive, be something extrodinarily unique that has not been put into practice previously, and having this game mode along side whatever else the game already offers, would give players that are a bit less competitive a chance to just have fun, unlock more customization options, rank up etc. Obviously this would take a ton of work and I'm sure you guys have your hands tied, when i started of thinking of this the past few weeks, and instead of pitching this to other game devs I know or projects, my mind almost immediately shifted back to multiversus. I think this would be a successful idea in any platform fighter that is good if done well, but what sets multiversus aside, is already having the unique perks system, having an emphasis on squads already, and last but not least, THE IPS GO CRAZY tony.
Just picture a battle royale where different wings of the map tie into the multiversus lore already, there are portals maybe part of the map is a scooby doo dimension, you're fighting against the cyber chase monster boss and the entire metroidvania level is designed like the levels from scooby doo and the cyber chase, along with tons of pve enemy fodder to work through along the way. Casual players would absolutely love it, and the pvpve elements alongside seeing their favorite characters battling it out, in multiple worlds, multiple different storylines happening. It could be wild.
